How to map

Classes on the left come from the BRIDGE research dumps (sierra-moxon.github.io/bridge-research). Lanes in the middle are BASALT’s process abstractions — the same left-to-right flow as the schema explorer.

  • Drag a class onto the lane where it belongs.
  • Open BASALT classes in a lane and drop onto a specific class for a tighter mapping.
  • Click a class, then click a lane — works on trackpads and phones.
  • Mappings stay in this browser (localStorage) until you export them.
